# Question

Anything a listener says back to a [lecture](LECTURE.md). It might be a request to explain ("wait, why?"), a challenge ("is that right?"), or a word about pace ("slower", "skip this", "go back"). These are all the same kind of thing: the room talking, and the lecturer expected to hear it.

## Invariants

- **No question is dropped.** Every question is answered, put off out loud ("I'll come to that in two slides"), or declined out loud. A lecture never carries on as if it hadn't been asked.
- A question can be asked at any moment, including while the lecturer is speaking. When to take it is the lecturer's judgment. That it was heard is shown to the asker straight away.
- A question belongs to the moment it was asked: the [slide](SLIDE.md) that was showing and what had just been said. It's answered in that light.
- An answer might be a few words, a return to an earlier slide, or a new slide. A slide made for a question is kept with the [deck](DECK.md) and remembers the question.
- **"Is that right?" gets a check, not a defence.** When the lecturer was wrong, it says so, and the correction is kept against the slide it concerns.
- A question that goes beyond what the lecturer knows gets "I don't know", never an invented answer.
- A question is attributed to whoever asked it when they've said who they are, and never to anyone else.
- The lecturer can ask too. A check on whether something landed, and the reply to it, are kept the same way.
- Questions are kept with the lecture they were asked in.
